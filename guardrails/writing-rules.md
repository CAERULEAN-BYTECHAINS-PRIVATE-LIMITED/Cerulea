# Writing rules

Every artefact Cerulea produces follows these rules. Source comments.
Documents. Interface copy. Error messages. Generated application text.

## Sentence construction

1. Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Break compound sentences down.
2. Avoid "because". Use a full stop instead.
3. No em dashes anywhere.
4. No "X, not Y" constructions.
5. Affirmative phrasing. Prefer stating what happens over what does not.
6. Name the real subject. Avoid leading with a bare generic subject as a
   formula. Write "Cerulea records the event" rather than "The record is
   created".
7. Avoid "it" where the subject can be named.
8. No prideful, rude or rhetorical statements.
9. Simple language, always professional.

## Terminology

1. The word Blockchain is capitalised everywhere, in every artefact.
2. Every term is explained immediately before or immediately after first
   use. Never introduce a word without explaining it.
3. Never the words "configuration" or "configure" in Cerulea material.
   Describe the engineering workflow instead.
4. No etymology and no name justification unless asked.

## Structure

1. Headings and titles carry no full stop. Full stops appear only in
   paragraphs.
2. Numbered lists at the first level. Bullets for sub-levels.
3. In a numbered point, the lead term is bolded.
4. Body paragraphs share one size. Never shrink a paragraph to fit.

## Claims

1. No marketing language. No hype adjectives. No overpromising.
2. Every factual claim carries a real, reputable citation.
3. Performance figures are labelled as targets until measured.
4. Compliance wording, verbatim: "Controls implemented, certification not
   held."

## Error messages and interface copy

1. Every error names the fix.
2. Every technical control carries a plain sentence stating what the
   control does and what breaks without it.
3. Never expose an internal identifier to a non-technical user.
